# Human review guide — IT004 v1.1 Theory B1

Please review the proof PDF at actual A4 size. This is a design decision gate; no full-book conversion should begin until the reviewer approves the direction.

## Review sequence

1. Cover A — Editorial Schema: Does the semantic relation chain feel like an authored technical book cover? Is the hierarchy calm and legible?
2. Cover B — Formal Relational: Does the denser notation remain elegant rather than looking like a formula sheet?
3. Cover C — Minimal Structural: Does the reduced composition have enough identity without becoming generic?
4. Choose a cover direction only after comparing A/B/C. The proof intentionally does not choose a winner.

## Evaluate the page system

- Typography: Vietnamese accents, body comfort, heading scale, formula and code legibility.
- Page density: enough information to study from, with deliberate whitespace and no orphan headings.
- Color: restrained in print and on a laptop; contrast remains clear in grayscale.
- Diagrams: each figure explains a mechanism (DBMS layers, schema keys, RA trace, division, closure, decomposition).
- Tables: readable at A4, clear row/column hierarchy, no tiny cells or awkward splits.
- Formulas and symbols: π, σ, ρ, ⋈, ÷, →, X⁺, and Vietnamese text render cleanly.
- Code: readable and academically presented, with no fake terminal frame, clipping, or horizontal overflow.
- Header/footer: quiet, consistent, and not repetitive branding.
- Human-authored feel: restrained Vietnamese headings (Ý chính, Cách hiểu, Ví dụ, Nhận xét, Sai lầm thường gặp, Bài tập, Ghi nhớ, Khi làm bài, Cách kiểm tra kết quả) rather than promotional labels.

## Required reviewer decisions

Please record a decision on each point before a full-book redesign is authorized:

1. Cover A, B, or C.
2. Comfort of the serif body at actual A4 reading distance.
3. Overall page density and the balance of information with whitespace.
4. Whether cards and small labels feel editorial rather than like web UI badges.
5. Diagram density: enough mechanism to teach, without turning figures into information panels.
6. Whether this proof feels suitable as the foundation for a complete 80–100 page book.

## Approval question

Does one of the three cover directions and the interior page system provide a credible, comfortable foundation for converting the full theory handbook while preserving the current academic content? Record the selected cover and any required changes before Phase B2.
